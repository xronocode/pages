description: "Fixed a Linux-only bug from a macOS machine, without a local repro. Symptom: the Ctrl key could stay 'virtually stuck' after push-to-talk dictation in Handy on X11. The reporter's xinput trace pinned the root cause to xdotool --clearmodifiers re-pressing a stale modifier snapshot after multi-key sequences. The fix releases the modifier latch after each xdotool type call. The reporter verified the build on his X11 setup ('works great'), and the maintainer merged it, adding a hardening pass on top of the cleanup."
